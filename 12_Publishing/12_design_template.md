# Design Document: The Legend of Sparky
**Status:** Draft 1
**Author:** Grug

## 1. Core Concept
A short story about a firescale lizard who lives inside a computer GPU.

## 2. Tone & Style
- **Grug Voice:** Simple sentences. Metaphors (Tech = Magic).

## 3. Characters
- **Sparky:** The GPU. Loves to eat electricity.
- **Rorri:** The AI Model.
